## ACH auto-responder (built 2026-08-21)

**No Google Form** — Claude cannot create one: this session has no Forms or
Apps Script tool, and Drive's `create_file` only makes docs/sheets/slides.
Adobe was considered and dropped (design tools only, no form backend).

Routed around it instead. The invoice carries a `mailto:` link that opens the
client's mail app with a machine-readable subject — `ACH Request - Job 26-3011`.
A time-driven Apps Script (every 5 min) finds those in Gmail, sends the ACH
details to the address **on file for that job**, notifies the office, and
labels the thread so it cannot be processed twice. Files: `scratchpad/ach/`.

Better than the form version: nothing to host, nothing to keep alive, works in
every mail client, and the request itself lands in Michael's inbox as a record.

Keyed on **job number**, not invoice number — job numbers are known and appear
in every thread, which also sidesteps the unresolved invoice-number problem.

Jobs not listed in `JOBS` release nothing and get labelled `ACH/needs-review`.

Deliberate design point: the script ignores the email the requester types.
Sending bank details to an arbitrary typed address would turn the invoice into
a self-serve harvester for the routing/account numbers — the standard BEC
pattern. Unrecognised invoice number → nothing sent, office alerted. Typed
address mismatches the one on file → details still go to the address on file,
office flagged.

Bank routing/account numbers were deliberately NOT looked up or recorded here.
They exist in Gmail as PDF attachments (the Chase direct-deposit info and
voided check sent 2026-07-14). Michael fills them into `Code.gs` locally so
they never enter this repo or a chat transcript.
